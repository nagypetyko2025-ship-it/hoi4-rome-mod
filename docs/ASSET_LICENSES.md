# Asset licences

All art in this mod is our own (generated by the scripts in `tools/`) or CC0. Nothing is copied from other mods.

## Leader portraits (`gfx/leaders/ante/*.dds`)
Drawn procedurally by `tools/portraits.py` (our own code, no external images): simple painted-style busts in the
dress of each people. Own work, released under CC0.

## Flags (`gfx/flags/`)
Painted procedurally by `tools/world.py` / `tools/build.py`. Own work, CC0.
- Tech icons (`gfx/interface/technologies/ante_*.dds`): own procedural art (tools/tech.py), CC0.
